## POST 2: Product Capabilities

How does your team track attendance across different work sites? 🌍

With **BuildOrbit**, we've taken workforce management to the next level. Our platform doesn't just record time—it verifies it. 

Key capabilities we're proud of:
✅ **Geofenced Attendance:** Utilizing HTML5 Geolocation and WebRTC, employees can check-in via their self-service portal only when verified within the 100m radius of their assigned site.
✅ **Unified Leave & Payroll:** When an unpaid leave is approved, BuildOrbit automatically calculates the deduction based on the daily rate and updates the draft payroll instantly.
✅ **Integrated CRM:** Manage your client lead pipelines directly alongside your workforce operations.
✅ **Dynamic Analytics:** Visualize attendance trends, payroll costs, and sales funnels through real-time, interactive dashboards.

BuildOrbit is designed to bring security, accuracy, and efficiency to your daily operations.

## POST 3: Product/Build Journey

Building a complete HR platform is much more than creating a beautiful interface. It's about modeling complex, real-world business logic. 💻🚀

During the development of **BuildOrbit**, our core challenge was ensuring absolute data integrity across interconnected modules. 

A single approved leave request doesn't just change a status. It deducts from an employee's annual balance, updates their attendance record for those specific dates, and dynamically recalculates their net salary for the monthly draft payroll—all while maintaining a strict audit trail and notifying the user.

By leveraging Next.js, Prisma, PostgreSQL, and a robust role-based architecture, we've engineered a platform that handles this complexity securely behind the scenes, offering a seamless and premium experience for the end-user.

Building software that solves real business problems is what drives us forward.
